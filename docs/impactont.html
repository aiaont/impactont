TemporalLocation

IRI: https://w3id.org/impactont#TemporalLocation
SKOSDefinition
The point or period in time at or during which a thing occurs, expressed in terms of the OWL-Time ontology.
Comments
A point in time is a single time:Instant, while a period in time is defined by two distinct time:Instants (typically using the time:ProperInterval class).
In Range Of
:hasTemporalLocation
